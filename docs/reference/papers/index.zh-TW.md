# 里程碑論文

精選的閱讀清單。每筆條目包括：作者（年份）、標題——以及它為何重要。

## 基礎

- McCulloch & Pitts (1943), "A Logical Calculus of the Ideas Immanent in Nervous Activity"——第一個形式化的神經元
- Turing (1950), "Computing Machinery and Intelligence"——模仿遊戲
- Rosenblatt (1958), "The Perceptron"——一台會學習的機器
- Shannon (1948), "A Mathematical Theory of Communication"——資訊理論
- Rumelhart, Hinton & Williams (1986), "Learning representations by back-propagating errors"——反向傳播的普及
- Hopfield (1982), "Neural networks and physical systems with emergent collective computational abilities"——聯想記憶
- Pearl (1988), *Probabilistic Reasoning in Intelligent Systems*——貝氏網路
- Cortes & Vapnik (1995), "Support-vector networks"
- Breiman (2001), "Random Forests"；以及 "Statistical Modeling: The Two Cultures"
