# Landmark Papers

A curated reading list. Each entry: authors (year), title — why it matters.

## Foundations

- McCulloch & Pitts (1943), "A Logical Calculus of the Ideas Immanent in Nervous Activity" — the first formal neuron
- Turing (1950), "Computing Machinery and Intelligence" — the imitation game
- Rosenblatt (1958), "The Perceptron" — a learning machine
- Shannon (1948), "A Mathematical Theory of Communication" — information theory
- Rumelhart, Hinton & Williams (1986), "Learning representations by back-propagating errors" — backprop popularised
- Hopfield (1982), "Neural networks and physical systems with emergent collective computational abilities" — associative memory
- Pearl (1988), *Probabilistic Reasoning in Intelligent Systems* — Bayesian networks
- Cortes & Vapnik (1995), "Support-vector networks"
- Breiman (2001), "Random Forests"; and "Statistical Modeling: The Two Cultures"

## Deep learning

- LeCun et al. (1998), "Gradient-based learning applied to document recognition" — LeNet
- Hochreiter & Schmidhuber (1997), "Long Short-Term Memory"
- Hinton, Osindero & Teh (2006), "A fast learning algorithm for deep belief nets"
- Krizhevsky, Sutskever & Hinton (2012), "ImageNet Classification with Deep Convolutional Neural Networks" — AlexNet
- Mikolov et al. (2013), word2vec
- Kingma & Welling (2013), "Auto-Encoding Variational Bayes" — VAE
- Goodfellow et al. (2014), "Generative Adversarial Nets"
- Srivastava et al. (2014), "Dropout"
- Bahdanau, Cho & Bengio (2014), "Neural Machine Translation by Jointly Learning to Align and Translate" — attention
- Kingma & Ba (2014), "Adam"
- Ioffe & Szegedy (2015), "Batch Normalization"
- He et al. (2016), "Deep Residual Learning for Image Recognition" — ResNet
- Vaswani et al. (2017), "Attention Is All You Need" — Transformer
- Devlin et al. (2018), BERT
- Radford et al. (2019), GPT-2; Brown et al. (2020), "Language Models are Few-Shot Learners" — GPT-3
- Kaplan et al. (2020) and Hoffmann et al. (2022) — scaling laws, Chinchilla
- Dosovitskiy et al. (2020), "An Image is Worth 16x16 Words" — ViT
- Ho, Jain & Abbeel (2020), "Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models"
- Radford et al. (2021), CLIP
- Rombach et al. (2022), latent diffusion
- Ouyang et al. (2022), InstructGPT — RLHF for instruction following
- Bai et al. (2022), "Constitutional AI"
- Wei et al. (2022), "Chain-of-Thought Prompting"
- Rafailov et al. (2023), DPO
- Gu & Dao (2023), Mamba
- DeepSeek-AI (2025), DeepSeek-R1 — reasoning via reinforcement learning

## Reinforcement learning

- Sutton (1988), "Learning to predict by the methods of temporal differences"
- Watkins & Dayan (1992), Q-learning
- Tesauro (1995), TD-Gammon
- Mnih et al. (2015), "Human-level control through deep reinforcement learning" — DQN
- Silver et al. (2016, 2017, 2018) — AlphaGo, AlphaGo Zero, AlphaZero
- Schulman et al. (2017), PPO
- Schrittwieser et al. (2020), MuZero
- Hafner et al. (2023/2025), DreamerV3

## Brain, mind and AI

- Hubel & Wiesel (1959, 1962) — receptive fields in visual cortex
- Marr (1982), *Vision*
- Rescorla & Wagner (1972) — prediction-error learning in conditioning
- Rumelhart, McClelland & PDP Group (1986), *Parallel Distributed Processing*
- Fodor & Pylyshyn (1988), "Connectionism and cognitive architecture: a critical analysis"
- McClelland, McNaughton & O'Reilly (1995) — complementary learning systems
- Olshausen & Field (1996) — sparse coding yields V1 receptive fields
- Schultz, Dayan & Montague (1997), "A Neural Substrate of Prediction and Reward"
- Rao & Ballard (1999) — predictive coding in visual cortex
- Doya (1999), "What are the computations of the cerebellum, the basal ganglia and the cerebral cortex?"
- Friston (2010), "The free-energy principle: a unified brain theory?"
- Yamins et al. (2014) — performance-optimised CNNs predict IT cortex
- Lake, Salakhutdinov & Tenenbaum (2015) — human-level concept learning
- Lillicrap et al. (2016) — feedback alignment
- Hassabis et al. (2017), "Neuroscience-Inspired Artificial Intelligence"
- Lake et al. (2017), "Building machines that learn and think like people"
- Kirkpatrick et al. (2017) — elastic weight consolidation
- Wang et al. (2018) — prefrontal cortex as a meta-RL system
- Richards et al. (2019), "A deep learning framework for neuroscience"
- Dabney et al. (2020) — distributional coding of reward in dopamine
- Schrimpf et al. (2021) — language models predict human language-network activity
- Willett et al. (2021, 2023) — handwriting and speech BCIs
- Butlin, Long et al. (2023) — consciousness indicators for AI
- Binz & Schulz (2023) — cognitive psychology of GPT-3
- Zador et al. (2023), "Catalyzing next-generation AI through NeuroAI"
