# People and Research Labs

A short guide to people and groups whose work shaped modern AI. It is a starting point for finding who to read, not a ranking. Each entry says what the person or group is known for and links to the topic page where the work is explained. The claims are checked against the source named in each entry.

## Foundations of deep learning

- **Yoshua Bengio, Geoffrey Hinton and Yann LeCun** shared the 2018 ACM A.M. Turing Award, announced in 2019, for "conceptual and engineering breakthroughs that have made deep neural networks a critical component of computing" ([ACM SIGAI](https://sigai.acm.org/aimatters/blog/2019/04/01/472/)). See [Deep Learning](../../theory/deep-learning/index.md) for the methods they developed.
- **John Hopfield and Geoffrey Hinton** shared the 2024 Nobel Prize in Physics for foundational work that enables machine learning with artificial neural networks ([Lindau Nobel Laureate Meetings](https://www.lindau-nobel.org/news-nobel-prize-in-physics-2024/)). For the brain-related side of this work, see [Brain & Mind](../../brain-mind/index.md).

## Vision and data

- **Fei-Fei Li** is the last of six authors, with Jia Deng, Wei Dong, Richard Socher, Li-Jia Li and Kai Li, of the 2009 CVPR paper that introduced ImageNet as a large-scale hierarchical image database ([CVPR 2009, mlanthology](https://mlanthology.org/cvpr/2009/deng2009cvpr-imagenet)). See [Computer Vision](../../theory/models/computer-vision/index.md) and [Datasets](../datasets/index.md).

## Research labs and institutes

- **Google DeepMind** was formed when DeepMind and the Brain team of Google Research were combined into one unit, announced in April 2023 ([Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/announcing-google-deepmind/)). For the wider timeline, see [History of AI](../../start/history/index.md).
- **Mila, the Quebec Artificial Intelligence Institute**, is based in Montreal. The institute's own page says Yoshua Bengio founded a research lab in 1993, and that the institute took its current form in 2017 through a collaboration between Université de Montréal and McGill University ([Mila](https://mila.quebec/en/mila/)).

## How to use this page

- Start with the award and prize winners above for the foundations, then read the topic pages they link to.
- Check the most recent work of any lab or person in the primary source, since positions and affiliations change.
- Add a person or group only with a verified source, and link to the topic page where their work is explained.
