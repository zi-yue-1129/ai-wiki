# Courses & Books

Free or openly licensed resources are marked **(free)**. See also the [Open-Source Resource Survey](../../start/open-source-survey/index.md).

## Books

### General and introductory
- Russell & Norvig, *Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach*
- Mitchell, *Artificial Intelligence: A Guide for Thinking Humans*
- Christian, *The Alignment Problem*

### Machine learning and deep learning
- Zhang et al., *Dive into Deep Learning* (free)
- Prince, *Understanding Deep Learning* (free PDF)
- Goodfellow, Bengio & Courville, *Deep Learning* (free online)
- Bishop & Bishop, *Deep Learning: Foundations and Concepts*
- Murphy, *Probabilistic Machine Learning* (drafts free)
- Hastie, Tibshirani & Friedman, *The Elements of Statistical Learning* (free)
- Sutton & Barto, *Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction* (free)
- Deisenroth, Faisal & Ong, *Mathematics for Machine Learning* (free)
- Raschka, *Build a Large Language Model (From Scratch)*
- Huyen, *AI Engineering*

### Neuroscience and cognition
- Kandel et al., *Principles of Neural Science*
- Dayan & Abbott, *Theoretical Neuroscience*
- Gerstner et al., *Neuronal Dynamics* (free online)
- Marr, *Vision*
- Lindsay, *Models of the Mind*
- Hawkins, *A Thousand Brains*
- Clark, *Surfing Uncertainty*
- Dehaene, *How We Learn*
- Kahneman, *Thinking, Fast and Slow*
- Griffiths, Chater & Tenenbaum, *Bayesian Models of Cognition*
- Seth, *Being You*

### Society
- Barocas, Hardt & Narayanan, *Fairness and Machine Learning* (free)
- Crawford, *Atlas of AI*
- Acemoglu & Johnson, *Power and Progress*
- Russell, *Human Compatible*
- Hendrycks, *Introduction to AI Safety, Ethics, and Society* (free)
