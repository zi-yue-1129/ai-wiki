# 課程與書籍

每個條目都連結到該課程或書籍自己的頁面。課程頁面明示免費的，標示為**（免費）**；其餘條目不主張免費。另見[開源資源調查](../../start/open-source-survey/index.md)。

## 書籍

### 通論與入門
- Russell & Norvig，*Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach*
- Mitchell，*Artificial Intelligence: A Guide for Thinking Humans*
- Christian，*The Alignment Problem*

### 機器學習與深度學習
- Zhang 等人，[*Dive into Deep Learning*](https://d2l.ai/)（《動手學深度學習》，線上免費）
- Prince，*Understanding Deep Learning*
- Goodfellow、Bengio 與 Courville，[*Deep Learning*](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/)（線上免費）
- Bishop & Bishop，*Deep Learning: Foundations and Concepts*
- Murphy，[*Probabilistic Machine Learning*](https://probml.github.io/pml-book/book1.html)（草稿免費，CC BY-NC-ND）
- Hastie、Tibshirani 與 Friedman，*The Elements of Statistical Learning*
- Sutton & Barto，[*Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction*](http://incompleteideas.net/book/the-book-2nd.html)
- Deisenroth、Faisal 與 Ong，[*Mathematics for Machine Learning*](https://mml-book.github.io/)（PDF 免費）
- Raschka，*Build a Large Language Model (From Scratch)*
- Huyen，*AI Engineering*

### 神經科學與認知
- Kandel 等人，*Principles of Neural Science*
- Dayan & Abbott，*Theoretical Neuroscience*
- Gerstner 等人，[*Neuronal Dynamics*](https://neuronaldynamics.epfl.ch/)（線上免費）
- Marr，*Vision*
- Lindsay，*Models of the Mind*
- Hawkins，*A Thousand Brains*
- Clark，*Surfing Uncertainty*
- Dehaene，*How We Learn*
- Kahneman，*Thinking, Fast and Slow*（《快思慢想》）
- Griffiths、Chater 與 Tenenbaum，*Bayesian Models of Cognition*
- Seth，*Being You*

### 社會
- Barocas、Hardt 與 Narayanan，[*Fairness and Machine Learning*](https://fairmlbook.org/)（線上免費）
- Crawford，*Atlas of AI*
- Acemoglu & Johnson，*Power and Progress*
- Russell，*Human Compatible*
- Hendrycks 等人，*Introduction to AI Safety, Ethics, and Society*
