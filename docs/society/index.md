## Risk landscape

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Present harms | Bias and discrimination, misinformation and deepfakes, privacy violations, unsafe advice, labour exploitation in data work |
| Misuse | Fraud, cyberattacks, bio/chem weapons uplift, surveillance, election manipulation |
| Accidents and reliability | Hallucinations in high-stakes settings, autonomous-system failures, reward hacking |
| Structural | Concentration of power, job disruption, erosion of skills, information ecosystem degradation |
| Loss of control | Highly capable systems pursuing goals misaligned with human intentions |

The *International AI Safety Report* (2025), led by Yoshua Bengio with contributions from experts nominated by 30 countries and international organisations, gives a consensus-oriented overview of capabilities and risks.
