# Knowledge Map

Every topic in the wiki, drawn from the pages themselves. The map opens on the five sections. Click a section to see its groups, then click a group to see its topics. Each topic is a circle coloured by its section, and a hollow circle marks a draft page. A line joins two topics that point to each other, either as a related topic or through a link in the text, so the map shows how fields such as medicine, law or robotics connect to the models and ideas they use.

The map is generated when the site is built, so it updates whenever a page is added, linked or completed.

## How to use it

- **Move around**: drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, and use the buttons above the map to zoom in, zoom out or fit the map to the screen.
- **Full screen**: the button in the toolbar fills the screen with the map. Press Esc to leave.
- **Find a topic**: type in the search box. Choosing a result opens its group and centres the topic.
- **Labels**: names appear when you zoom in or point at a circle. Selecting a circle highlights the topics it links to.
- **List**: the list under the map gives the same topics as plain links, for reading or searching on a phone.
