# Knowledge Map

Every topic in the wiki, drawn from the pages themselves. A topic is a circle, and it is coloured by how complete its page is. A line joins two topics that point to each other, either as a related topic or through a link in the text, so the map shows how fields such as medicine, law or robotics connect to the models and ideas they use.

The map is generated when the site is built, so it updates whenever a page is added, linked or completed. Click any circle to open its page, and open a section below for a closer view.

## How to read it

- **Clusters** are sections. Topics are pulled toward the section they belong to, but links between sections pull them together.
- **Dense knots** are topics that many others refer to, such as the mathematics and learning-theory pages.
- **Isolated circles** are topics that no other page links to yet. Adding a related topic to their front matter, or a link in the text, connects them.
